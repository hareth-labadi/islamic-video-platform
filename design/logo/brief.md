# New logo — brief (November 2026)

## The job
A new symbol for the Islamic lessons video platform (series, shorts, Q&A with voice answers, several sheikhs;
Arabic, French and Taqbaylit). **The current name is not used**: the mark must stand on its own, so it is
designed as a name-independent symbol. The wordmark and lockups are built once the new name is chosen.

## Audience
Algerian and francophone Muslims, many of them young and arriving from TikTok shorts; families; students
who follow a whole series. Reads right-to-left and left-to-right.

## Adjectives → visual cues
| Adjective | Cue |
| --- | --- |
| Trustworthy (sound, sourced knowledge) | calm geometry, nothing gimmicky, roots in real Islamic craft |
| Clear | one idea, one silhouette, works at 16 px |
| Warm | soft rhythm, paper-and-ink feel of the v4 site |
| Living / modern | it is a *video* platform: the play idea must be there, but not as a stock play button |
| Rooted in the Maghreb | regional architecture and script, not generic Gulf imagery |

## Constraints
- Must sit inside the existing v4 design system (paper `#f4f1ea`, ink `#161616`, series green `#0b5d45`,
  accent gold `#f2b035`) — the team likes the current site.
- Favicon, TikTok/Telegram avatar and app icon matter as much as the header.
- No figurative living beings; no Qur'anic text or names of Allah inside the mark (it gets printed, cropped, discarded).
- One colour, reversed on ink, and 16 px must all work.

## Category clichés (avoid unless given a fresh form)
Crescent and star · dome and minaret silhouettes · open book / Qur'an · mihrab with a lamp · pen nib ·
the name in calligraphy inside a seal · eight-pointed star · green + gold by default ·
video: play triangle in a rounded screen, film reel, camera.
The current mark uses four of these at once (mihrab, nib, open book, green + gold).

## Word map — intersections
- *play* × *Arabic script*: the three dots of ش/ث are a triangle; turned, they point forward.
- *play* × *series* × *Maghrebi architecture*: an arcade receding in perspective narrows to a point.
- *play* × *degrees* (يرفع الله الذين آمنوا منكم والذين أوتوا العلم درجات — 58:11) × *minbar*: steps.
- *halaqa* (circle of learning) × *screen*; *nuqta* as the unit of calligraphy; *mashrabiya* = a screen.

## Round 1 (built in `round1/`, generated by `src/make_concepts.py`)
Shown: **A Qalam** (play drawn with a broad-nib reed pen), **B Riwaq** (arcade of Maghrebi horseshoe arches
receding into a play point — recommended), **C Darajat** (minbar steps).

Tried and dropped:
- *Nuqat* — three rhombic dots (ش) turned into play: every arrangement that reads as play drifts toward
  Mitsubishi (three rhombi) or Tidal (diamond cluster); the safe one reads as falling squares.
- *Ghayth* — a raindrop on its side (Bukhari 79): reads as a rotated map pin; not ownable.
- *Lisan* — the envelope flap of a closed Arabic manuscript (it points right because Arabic books open on the
  left): in one colour only the gap line shows, which reads as a terminal prompt `>` in a box.

Test notes: all three pass the audit (1 colour, no text, 11–28 anchors). Riwaq's arches turn to noise at 16 px →
needs a small-size cut (one arch, or the solid triangle) for the favicon. Qalam sits closest to GoCD's chevron on
the shelf test.

## Chosen: Qalam (November 2026)
The owner picked **A Qalam**. Final files in `final/` (generated by `src/make_final.py`):
- `qalam.svg` brand green `#0b5d45` (on paper) · `qalam-gold.svg` gold `#f2b035` (on ink) · `qalam-tight.svg`
  cropped for UI · `qalam-small.svg` heavier nib for 16–48 px · `qalam-icon.svg` gold on a green tile.
- `web/` favicon.ico (16/32/48, from the tile), PNG icons, black/white/mono variants.
- On the site: `src/components/ui/BrandMark.tsx` (inline SVG, `currentColor`), `src/app/favicon.ico`,
  `src/app/icon.svg`, `src/app/apple-icon.png`; copies for other uses in `public/brand/`.

Still open: the wordmark (the site name is still typed text next to the mark) and a trademark search.

## Assumptions (not confirmed by the owner)
- New name still open; symbol-only first round.
- Palette stays close to the site's; colour is decided after a direction is chosen.
